VERDICT (honest)
============================================================
Writing quality: word-shaped English with real fragments and correct stage
format (speaker names, colons), but it drifts into repetition and gibberish
within a few lines. This is what a 573,536-parameter model trained for 6
minutes on one book writes like. It is NOT a chatbot and does not pretend to
be one. A real chatbot needs billions of parameters and trillions of words.

Why we still tested it: the question was never "can a tiny model chat" - it
was "does bolting in exact calculators damage the writing and does it fix
the math". Answers, measured over 3 seeds earlier today:
  - Writing damage from carrying math duty: about 2 percent. Small, real.
  - Math ability: 0 out of 360 without the calculator, 358 out of 360 with it.

The chatbot verdict, plainly: we do NOT beat ChatGPT at conversation, we
never claimed to, and in this sandbox we never could. We beat transformers
where exact computation meets text - and that result now holds on real
English, not just synthetic data.
