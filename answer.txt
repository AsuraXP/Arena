2. NEW TEST I JUST RAN TODAY (this is the piece you asked about)
   Question: our math-machine is a "calculator bolted inside a tiny AI".
   Does that work when the AI is trained on REAL English text?
   And does carrying the calculator make its normal writing worse?

   I downloaded 112KB of real Shakespeare into the sandbox and ran the trial.
   It took 5 attempts. Two real bugs were found and fixed (best one: the
   Shakespeare corpus contains ZERO digit characters, so all numbers were being
   silently deleted before training even started).

   FINAL RESULT (3 runs, different random seeds):
   - Hybrid (AI + calculator): 358/360 exact on embedded math, up to
     20-digit numbers. That is 99.4 percent.
   - Same AI without the calculator: 0/360. Zero percent.
   - So: same tiny AI, bolt the calculator on, math goes from 0 to 100.
   - Writing quality: carrying math duty costs a SMALL real price, about
     2 percent worse prediction quality. Not free, but tiny.
     (One earlier single run made it look free. More seeds showed a small
     real cost. I record that honestly.)
